Automated Azure Document Intelligence Invoice Processor

  • Python

Developed an automation pipeline that processed thousands of monthly invoices by extracting line items from email attachments using a custom Azure Document Intelligence neural extraction model. Integrated with Dynamics 365 Business Central through a custom AL extension that enabled interactive retraining of the model. This system eliminated hours of manual data entry each week and improved accuracy in inventory tracking.
